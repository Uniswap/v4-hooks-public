# HookRoles

[Git Source](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-hooks-public/blob/3d5b2620fe2b9ba7f01645160e1b841cf585c425/src/base/HookRoles.sol)

**Inherits:** AccessControlUpgradeable

**Title:** HookRoles

AccessControl base + role identifiers shared by the upgradeable hooks and their
configuration mixins.

<i>

Role hierarchy: DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE is the role admin of CONFIG_MANAGER_ROLE,
POOL_INITIALIZER_ROLE, and itself — it alone grants and revokes roles. The two
operational roles hold only their own operational powers, not role administration.

</i>

**Note:**

- **security-contact:** security@uniswap.org
